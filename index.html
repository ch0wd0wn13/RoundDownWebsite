Frequently Asked Questions

Does RoundDown choose investments for me?

No. RoundDown only transfers money. You choose and manage investments through your brokerage separately.

Does RoundDown hold my money?

The concept moves money from your checking account to your existing brokerage. RoundDown is not intended to hold your money.

Can I choose my brokerage?

The concept is designed for your existing brokerage. Supported connections will be confirmed before launch.

What happens if my balance is low?

A transfer never exceeds the amount available above your balance floor. If no amount is available, it moves nothing.

Can I change my settings?

Yes. The concept gives you control over the Round Down level, minimum balance, maximum transfer, and schedule.

Is this available yet?

Not yet. This is a market-research landing page and interactive concept demo, with no live financial connections.
